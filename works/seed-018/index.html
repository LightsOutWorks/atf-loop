GATE SHIFT

Glyphs drift down three lanes. Tap a lane's gate to catch what's passing through it — but each gate's accepted color reshuffles over time, so check the label before you commit.

Mouse/touch: tap a gate at the bottom of its lane. Keyboard: Left/Right to pick a gate, Enter/Space to catch. Matching the gate's current color builds your combo; a wrong color or a missed match resets it.
