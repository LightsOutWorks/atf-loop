CIRCUIT ECHO

A circuit-recall toy: watch the pulse sequence light up the grid, then tap the same nodes back in order — a wrong tap forces a replay, and each cleared pattern grows one step longer.

Mouse/touch: watch the lit sequence, then tap the nodes in the same order. Keyboard: Arrow keys to move the cursor, Enter/Space to tap the selected node during your turn. Matching the full pattern extends it by one node; a wrong tap resets your turn and replays the pattern.
