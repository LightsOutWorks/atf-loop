GRID PATROL

A security-grid patrol toy: tap the numbered checkpoints in ascending order before time runs out — misreading the sequence breaks your streak, and each cleared grid fills in denser than the last.

Mouse/touch: tap the checkpoint showing the NEXT number in the HUD. Keyboard: Arrow keys to move the cursor between checkpoints, Enter/Space to tap the selected one. Correct order builds your combo; a wrong number resets it.
